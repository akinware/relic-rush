Relic Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Relic Rush is a game made by Akinware (contact: akinakcam@akinware.live). This policy explains what data the game and its partners handle.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress (best score, gold, gems, boosters, upgrades, unlocked explorers, settings) is stored only on your device. There are no accounts, and we do not run a server that receives this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data deletes it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for example to revive or double your gold) and full-screen ads between runs. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, app interactions and approximate location. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are used; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

The web version shows placeholder ads only, unless Google H5 Games Ads are enabled, in which case the same Google policies apply.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases such as gem packs or "Remove Ads" are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation of which item was bought so it can be added to your game.

4. Hosting

The web version and this page are hosted by Vercel Inc. When you open them, Vercel processes technical data (such as IP address, time and requested page) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

5. Children

Relic Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Legal basis and your rights (EU/EEA)

Ads are personalised only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); non-personalised ads and purchases rely on our legitimate interest in funding and operating the game and on performing the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing and to complain to a data-protection authority. Because we do not hold data about you ourselves, requests about ad data are best directed to Google; we will gladly help — just email us.

7. Changes

We will update this page when the game changes how data is handled. The date at the top shows the latest version.
